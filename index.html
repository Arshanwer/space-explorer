Where the pictures come from

  • Sun, Earth, Saturn's rings and the Milky Way: Solar System Scope (CC BY 4.0), made from NASA pictures.
  • The Moon, the other planets, their moons, Pluto and Ceres: Real Solar System texture pack by the KSP-RO team (CC BY-NC-SA), made from NASA/JPL pictures.
  • Venus's clouds: Planet Pixel Emporium.
  • Space Station, Hubble, Vesta, Eros, Bennu and Itokawa shapes: NASA 3D Resources.
  • Phobos and Deimos shapes: NASA/JPL-Caltech.
  • Stars: Yale Bright Star Catalogue. It has every star you can see with your eyes.
  • Stars in 3D: the HYG database by David Nash (CC BY-SA 4.0), made from the Hipparcos, Yale and Gliese catalogues.
  • The shape of the Milky Way's arms: measurements by Reid and others (2019).
  • The real picture of our black hole: made from the Event Horizon Telescope's measurements (EHT Collaboration, public data) with the telescope team's own picture-making program.
  • The stars racing round the black hole, and its weight: the GRAVITY team (2022).
  • Hubble pictures (NASA and ESA): Orion Nebula (M. Robberto and the Hubble Orion Treasury Project Team), Pillars of Creation (J. Hester and P. Scowen), Mystic Mountain (M. Livio and the Hubble 20th Anniversary Team), Crab Nebula (J. Hester and A. Loll) and its middle (J. Hester and M. Weisskopf), Ring Nebula, Horsehead Nebula, Sombrero and M87 (Hubble Heritage Team, STScI/AURA), Helix Nebula (NOAO, the Hubble Helix Nebula Team, M. Meixner and T.A. Rector), N159 (ESA/Hubble, CC BY 4.0), the exploded star E0102 (J. Banovetz and D. Milisavljevic), Whirlpool Galaxy (S. Beckwith and the Hubble Heritage Team), Pinwheel Galaxy (K. Kuntz, F. Bresolin, J. Trauger, J. Mould, Y.-H. Chu and STScI, with CFHT and NOAO), Triangulum Galaxy (M. Durbin, J. Dalcanton and B.F. Williams), Centaurus A (Hubble Heritage and ESA/Hubble), and the Hubble Ultra Deep Field (S. Beckwith and the HUDF Team).
  • James Webb Space Telescope pictures (NASA, ESA, CSA and STScI): the Cosmic Cliffs in the Carina Nebula, the Southern Ring Nebula and the Tarantula Nebula.
  • The Andromeda Galaxy photo: Guillermo Ferla (Unsplash).
  • The first picture of a black hole (M87): made from the Event Horizon Telescope's public measurements, like the one of our own black hole.
  • Star patterns: Stellarium's sky cultures (the modern star figures and the Māori sky culture). Where the Sun, Moon and planets are: NASA JPL's planet elements (E.M. Standish) and Paul Schlyter's Sun and Moon method.
  • 11,000 galaxies in their real places: Celestia's galaxy catalogue (GPL), built from W. Steinicke's Revised NGC/IC catalogue, Karachentsev and others (2004), and NASA/IPAC's NED distances.
