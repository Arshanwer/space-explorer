Where the pictures come from

  • Sun, Earth, Saturn's rings and the Milky Way: Solar System Scope (CC BY 4.0), made from NASA pictures.
  • The Moon, the other planets, their moons, Pluto and Ceres: Real Solar System texture pack by the KSP-RO team (CC BY-NC-SA), made from NASA/JPL pictures.
  • Venus's clouds: Planet Pixel Emporium.
  • Space Station, Hubble, Vesta, Eros, Bennu and Itokawa shapes: NASA 3D Resources.
  • Phobos and Deimos shapes: NASA/JPL-Caltech.
  • Stars: Yale Bright Star Catalogue. It has every star you can see with your eyes.
  • Stars in 3D: the HYG database by David Nash (CC BY-SA 4.0), made from the Hipparcos, Yale and Gliese catalogues.
  • The shape of the Milky Way's arms: measurements by Reid and others (2019).
  • The real picture of our black hole: made from the Event Horizon Telescope's measurements (EHT Collaboration, public data) with the telescope team's own picture-making program.
  • The stars racing round the black hole, and its weight: the GRAVITY team (2022).
